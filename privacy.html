HushNotes Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 · Publisher: Reassurix

In short: HushNotes processes your audio, transcripts and notes entirely on your own PC. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your recordings, transcripts, notes or any personal data.

What HushNotes does with your data

  • Audio and video files you open, and recordings you make (microphone and/or computer audio) are processed on your PC by a speech-recognition model that runs locally. They are never sent to us or to any cloud service.
  • Transcripts, notes and recordings are saved only in your Windows user profile (in the app's local data folder) and in any files you choose to export.
  • Settings (such as your chosen language and accuracy level) are stored locally on your PC.

Microphone and computer audio

HushNotes uses your microphone and your computer's audio output only while you are actively recording, after you press Record. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone. Please always get everyone's permission before recording a conversation or meeting.

Network use

HushNotes includes a speech model, so it works offline. The app connects to the internet only if you choose a larger accuracy level that is not yet on your PC; it then downloads that speech model (open-source Whisper models) from Hugging Face. No audio, text or personal information is sent during this download. Hugging Face may log standard technical data such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

Optional local AI notes

If you choose to write notes with a local AI engine you have installed yourself (for example Ollama, Microsoft Foundry Local or LM Studio), HushNotes sends the transcript only to that engine at a localhost address on your own PC. HushNotes refuses any address that is not on your PC.

What we collect

Nothing. HushNotes has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Microsoft may provide us with aggregated, anonymous Store statistics (such as install counts and crash reports) under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Deleting your data

Delete any session inside the app with the Delete button. Uninstalling HushNotes removes its local data folder. Files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Children

HushNotes is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
